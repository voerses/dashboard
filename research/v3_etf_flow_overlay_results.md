No ETF flow overlay variant passed the kill criteria. Reasons:

- **V1: Flow Z-Score Sizing**: dSharpe=-0.501 < 0, OOS expanding-window: only 4/23 positive dSharpe

**Recommendation**: The ETF flow signal has predictive power (R40 confirmed IC=+0.191 at 14d), but it does NOT improve V3 as a sizing overlay in the current form. Possible explanations:
- V3's existing overlays (positioning + VRP) may already capture the information in ETF flows indirectly
- The flow signal works at 14d horizon but V3 rebalances weekly, creating a mismatch
- 26 months is simply too short to detect a real improvement

**Next steps**:
- Investigate whether ETF flow is redundant with positioning (both measure institutional activity)
- Test a regime-conditional version: only apply ETF overlay during TIGHTENING regime (where R40 found the strongest signal)
- Wait for more data and re-test after Q4 2026
