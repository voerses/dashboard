# R155 -- Multi-Strategy Portfolio Combiner with Leverage Optimization

## Context
- Target: 300% return in last 12 months (2025-03-17 to 2026-03-17)
- BTC was DOWN ~10.8% in that period
- Individual strategies showed near-zero 12-month returns
- This research tests whether combining strategies with leverage can bridge the gap

## Methodology
- All signals computed on DAILY bars (1h aggregated to 1D)
- Signals use PREVIOUS day's values (shift by 1) -- no look-ahead bias
- Costs: 7 bps per side on entries/exits + daily funding from perp data
- EMA warm-up: 60 days skipped
- Data range: 2020-01-01 to 2026-03-17
- Last 12mo window: 2025-03-17 to 2026-03-17

## Conclusion

**Best last-12mo return: C: BTC+Alt Combo 1x at +5.6%**

Even with leverage and strategy combinations, the best result was +5.6%.
The 300% target appears unreachable with simple EMA trend-following on BTC/alts
in a period where BTC was down 10.8%. Possible next steps:
- More aggressive mean-reversion strategies
- Options-based strategies (gamma farming)
- Cross-exchange arbitrage
- Higher-frequency (intraday) signals

## Risk Warnings

- Leveraged strategies can lose more than the initial capital
- Kelly-optimal leverage assumes known distribution parameters -- estimation error is real
- Funding costs compound at higher leverage
- Alt basket has survivorship bias (we chose tokens that still exist)
- All results are backtested -- live performance will differ due to slippage, latency, liquidity
