# R150 -- Honest EMA Trend-Following Results

## Strategy
- **Trend**: Hourly EMA(168)/EMA(720) cross (no daily regime)
- **Pullback**: Price touches EMA(20) during active trend (min 72 bars after cross)
- **Confirmation**: MACD histogram direction, BB bandwidth < 95th pctile
- **Exit**: 3.0x ATR(48) trailing stop, 1.5x ATR breakeven ratchet
- **Costs**: 7 bps per side (4 taker + 3 slippage) + hourly funding
- **Sizing**: 25% equity per position, max 4 positions
- **Pullback cooldown**: 168h between pullback entries per direction

Last 12 months cutoff: 2025-03-28

## Notes
- No daily regime used (avoids look-ahead bias from daily bar forward-fill)
- Hourly EMA(168) ~ 1 week, EMA(720) ~ 1 month
- First 720 bars skipped for EMA warm-up
- Funding applied per-hour: longs pay positive funding, shorts collect
- BB squeeze relaxed to 95th pctile (vs 80th in s98)
- 168h cooldown between pullback entries
- Pullback requires 72 bars since last cross (trend established)
- ATR period = 48 (2 days) for wider stop that respects hourly noise
