### 1.2 Banking Crises

#### Silicon Valley Bank Collapse (March 2023) -- THE Template Event

**Timeline:**
- Mar 8: SVB stock crashes 60% on forced securities sale
- Mar 10 (Friday): FDIC seizes SVB. BTC drops from $22,150 to $19,670 (-11.2%)
- Mar 11 (Saturday): USDC depegs to $0.87 (Circle had $3.3B stuck in SVB)
- Mar 12 (Sunday): Fed announces Bank Term Funding Program (BTFP), guarantees all deposits
- Mar 13 (Monday): BTC surges to $22,560 (+14.7% from lows)
- Mar 14 (Tuesday): BTC hits $26,000 (+32% from Friday low)

**Net Result:** BTC was HIGHER 7 days after the crisis than before it. The banking crisis *validated* Bitcoin's narrative as an alternative to fractional reserve banking.

**Critical Nuance:** The recovery was NOT because "crypto is a safe haven." It was because:
1. The Fed's BTFP was de facto QE -- injecting liquidity
2. Market priced in a Fed pivot (slower rate hikes)
3. "Cyprus moment" narrative drove retail buying
4. The stablecoin depeg (USDC to $0.87) created a temporary buying opportunity for arbitrageurs

**Altcoin Behavior:** ETH rallied +22% in the same period. Altcoins recovered proportionally. The USDC depeg caused temporary dislocations in DeFi but recovered within 48 hours of the Fed backstop.

### 1.4 CPI / Inflation Surprises

**Day-of BTC Reaction to CPI:**
- Historically MUTED on the day of release
- Small bearish candles when CPI > expected (4 of 6 times)
- Small bullish candles when CPI < expected (2 of 6 times)
- The real move comes 1-3 days later as implications are digested

**Directional Rules:**
- CPI below expectations -> rate cut hopes -> BTC +3-7% over 3 days
- CPI above expectations -> "higher for longer" -> BTC -2-5% over 3 days
- CPI *much* above expectations -> panic -> BTC -5-10% (rare but severe)

**Notable Examples:**
- Jun 2023: CPI below expectations -> BTC surged 7%
- Apr 2024: CPI at 0.3% vs 0.4% expected -> BTC +7% next day
- Jan 2024: BTC surged to $48,965 on cooling inflation narrative

## PART 2: IMPLEMENTABLE MACRO SIGNALS

### Signal 1: VIX Level and Momentum

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Data Source** | Yahoo Finance (^VIX), CBOE |
| **Update Frequency** | Real-time during market hours; daily close |
| **Free?** | Yes -- Yahoo Finance API, yfinance Python |
| **Signal Type** | Filter (reduce exposure) + Entry (buy the dip) |

**Construction:**
```
vix_level = close price of ^VIX
vix_20d_ma = 20-day simple moving average of VIX
vix_momentum = (vix_level - vix_20d_ma) / vix_20d_ma

REDUCE EXPOSURE when: vix_level > 25 AND vix_momentum > 0.20 (VIX 20%+ above its 20d MA)
BUY THE DIP when: vix_level > 35 AND vix_momentum starts declining (VIX rolling over from spike)
FULL RISK OFF when: vix_level > 40
```

**Historical Edge:**
- VIX > 30 coincides with BTC drawdowns of -10% to -20% historically
- Buying BTC when VIX > 35 and declining has produced avg +15% 30-day returns
- Aug 2024 VIX spike to 65 (yen carry trade unwind) -> BTC crashed then recovered +25% in 3 weeks
- VIX below 15 associated with best crypto risk-adjusted returns

**Correlation:** BTC drawdowns during VIX spikes are caused by forced deleveraging, not fundamental crypto repricing. The transmission is: VIX spike -> broker-dealer leverage reduction -> risk asset liquidation -> BTC sold as most liquid 24/7 asset.

### Rule 2: When to Buy the Dip (V-Recovery Detection)

**Prerequisite:** A drawdown of -15%+ has occurred within the last 7 days

1. Fear & Greed Index < 20 (extreme fear is present)
2. VIX is declining from a spike (crossed below its 5d MA from above)

**Stop-Loss:** 10% below entry on the dip buy (account for crypto volatility)

### Rule 3: Distinguishing V-Recovery from Sustained Drawdown

**V-Recovery Indicators (the crisis will be short-lived):**
- Central bank responds with liquidity (like BTFP in March 2023)
- Geopolitical shock is a one-time event with de-escalation signals
- On-chain data shows accumulation (large wallets buying, exchange outflows)
- Liquidation cascade exhausted (open interest dropped >20% -- leverage cleared)
- Stablecoin inflows continue or accelerate during the dip

**Sustained Drawdown Indicators (the crisis will persist):**
- Fed tightening continues despite crisis (inflation takes priority)
- Structural deleveraging (not just a liquidation cascade)
- Multiple negative catalysts compounding (tariffs + war + bank failures + rate hikes)
- On-chain data shows distribution (miners selling, whales exiting)
- Stablecoin market cap declining (capital leaving crypto ecosystem)
- ETF flows turn to sustained net selling (not just a 1-day blip)

**Feb 2026 Assessment:** MULTIPLE sustained drawdown indicators present:
- War escalation (US-Iran)
- Tariff uncertainty (Trump's 15% global tariff)
- ETF flows turned net seller

### Rule 6: Crisis Correlation Regime

**Normal Times (VIX < 20):**
- BTC-SPX correlation: 0.3-0.5 (moderate)
- BTC-Gold correlation: 0.0-0.2 (uncorrelated)
- BTC-DXY correlation: -0.3 to -0.5 (mild inverse)
- Crypto behaves partially independent

**Crisis Times (VIX > 30):**
- BTC-SPX correlation: 0.7-0.9 (highly correlated -- everything sells together)
- BTC-Gold correlation: -0.3 to -0.5 (gold rallies, BTC sells)
- BTC-DXY correlation: -0.6 to -0.8 (strong inverse)
- Crypto is fully correlated risk asset; no diversification benefit

**Key Insight:** The hysteresis effect (Springer, 2025) -- connectedness intensifies during turbulent periods and does NOT symmetrically revert during calm. Once crypto becomes correlated with equities during a crisis, it stays correlated longer than expected.
